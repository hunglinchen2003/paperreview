1. LAF‑Stp XPs 結構差異（U‑形與 Bundle 兩種 LAF‑Stp XPs 的結構差異）

核心機制：U‑形載體的非對稱分支使其在結合 LAF 時形成 U‑形結構，降低脂質比例，提升靈活性與細胞內部穿透力；Bundle 載體的對稱分支則產生樹枝狀、體積較大的結構，較高的脂質含量增強了載藥容量與穩定性。

實驗亮點：通過調節 Stp:LAF 比例（1:2 vs 1:4）直接改變載體的分支對稱性與脂質含量，並利用形態學與功能測試證實兩種載體在細胞內部定位與藥物釋放速率上的差異。

臨床啟示：U‑形載體因其較低脂質含量與靈活結構，可能更適合於需要快速細胞內部穿透的靶向治療；Bundle 載體則因其高載藥容量與穩定性，適用於長時間釋放或高劑量藥物的應用。

2. Drp1 失活、海馬突觸功能與 Galectin-3 介導的神經炎症（神經元 Drp1 缺失下海馬星形細胞及小胶质细胞中 Galectin-3 表達變化及其與突觸功能的關聯）

核心機制：神經元 Drp1 缺失導致線粒體分裂失衡，造成能量供應不足與 ROS 產生升高，進而破壞突觸結構與功能；同時，突觸失衡激活星形細胞與小胶质细胞，Galectin‑3 表達上升，可能介導炎症訊號傳遞，進一步加劇神經元損傷。 實驗亮點：結合行為、電生理、超微結構與 glial 標誌的多層面評估，證明 Drp1 缺失在 HFD 背景下可顯著惡化海馬突觸功能與記憶表現；使用原代神經元模型進一步驗證 Drp1 直接影響細胞存活與突觸結構。 臨床啟示：維持 Drp1‑介導的線粒體動態可能是抵抗代謝性腦損傷的關鍵；Galectin‑3 作為 glial 炎症指標，或成為評估 HFD 相關認知障礙進展的生物標誌物。 研究限制：僅使用小鼠模型，缺乏人類資料；未進行 Drp1 或 Galectin‑3 的功能性恢復實驗；未直接測定 Galectin‑3 對突觸功能的機制。

3. Galectin‑3結合與抗纖維化（N‑乙酰半乳糖胺4,6‑O‑去硫化提升Galectin‑3結合，並抑制肺纖維化相關訊號）

  • 實驗亮點：結合合成、SPR、MD、細胞與動物模型的多層次驗證，證實結構修飾可直接影響蛋白結合與纖維化表現。
  • 臨床啟示：此三糖作為Galectin‑3/HMGB1雙重抑制劑，具備潛在的肺纖維化治療前景；若能進一步優化藥代動力學與安全性，或可作為新型抗纖維化藥物候選。

4. 心肌梗塞後心肌纖維化抑制（Galectin-3 與 TGF-β1/Smad3 途徑在心肌纖維化中的作用）

  • 核心機制：Iminostilbene 直接結合 Galectin‑3，抑制其在心肌成纖維細胞中的表達，進而阻斷 Galectin‑3 介導的 TGF‑β1/Smad3 信號通路，減少 ECM 蛋白（α‑SMA、Collagen I）的合成與沉積。 - 實驗亮點：採用 PAL‑CC‑ABPP 技術首次將小分子 Iminostilbene 的結合靶點定位於 Galectin‑3，並通過競爭實驗與 DARTS 進行功能驗證；同時結合體內（大鼠 MI 模型）與體外（TGF‑β1 刺激的 CFs）實驗，證明其抗纖維化效應。 - 臨床啟示：Galectin‑3 已被證實為心衰的預後指標，Iminostilbene 作為 Galectin‑3 抑制劑可作為新型心衰治療藩籬；其同時具備抗炎、抗氧化特性，可能在多重機制下提升心肌修復。 - 研究限制：僅在大鼠模型與體外細胞中驗證，缺乏人類臨床數據；未評估長期安全性與藥代動力學；Galectin‑3 之外的潛在靶點未進一步探討。

  • Bundle carriers display symmetric branching, resulting in a dendritic, bulkier molecular architecture with higher lipid content (Stp:LAF 1:4)
  • These structural differences translate into distinct targeting and drug‑delivery properties

2. Drp1 loss, hippocampal synaptic dysfunction and Galectin-3-mediated neuroinflammation (Altered Galectin-3 expression in astrocytes and microglia following neuronal Drp1 deletion and its impact on synaptic function)

Core Insight: Neuronal Drp1 loss disrupts mitochondrial fission, leading to energy deficits and ROS accumulation that compromise synaptic architecture; the resulting synaptic dysfunction triggers astrocyte and microglial activation, with up‑regulation of Galectin‑3 that may propagate inflammatory signaling and further neuronal injury. Methodology Highlights: Multi‑modal assessment—behavior, patch‑clamp, TEM, and glial markers—demonstrated that Drp1 deficiency under HFD exacerbates hippocampal synaptic deficits and memory impairment; primary neuron culture confirmed Drp1’s direct role in cell viability and synaptic puncta. Clinical Angle: Preserving Drp1‑dependent mitochondrial dynamics could protect against metabolic‑stress‑induced cognitive decline; Galectin‑3 may serve as a glial‑inflammation biomarker for HFD‑related neurodegeneration. Limitations: Mouse‑only data, no human validation; no rescue experiments for Drp1 or Galectin‑3; mechanistic link between Galectin‑3 and synaptic dysfunction remains indirect.

3. Galectin‑3 Binding & Antifibrosis (Enhanced Galectin‑3 binding via 4,6‑O‑desulfation and attenuation of fibrosis signaling)

  • Core Insight: 4,6‑O‑desulfation reduces steric hindrance in the carbohydrate recognition domain of Galectin‑3, enhancing affinity; simultaneous HMGB1 binding may dampen pro‑inflammatory signaling, attenuating TGF‑β/Smad pathways.
  • Methodology Highlights: Integrated synthetic chemistry, SPR, MD simulations, cell‑based assays, and an in vivo bleomycin fibrosis model to demonstrate structure‑function relationships.
  • Clinical Angle: The trisaccharide functions as a dual Galectin‑3/HMGB1 inhibitor with antifibrotic potential; further pharmacokinetic and safety optimization could pave the way for novel antifibrotic therapeutics.

4. Post-MI myocardial fibrosis suppression (Galectin-3 and its regulation of the TGF-β1/Smad3 pathway in myocardial fibrosis)

  • Core Insight: Iminostilbene binds Galectin‑3, down‑regulates its expression in cardiac fibroblasts, and blocks the Galectin‑3‑mediated TGF‑β1/Smad3 signaling cascade, thereby reducing ECM protein synthesis (α‑SMA, Collagen I). - Methodology Highlights: PAL‑CC‑ABPP identified Galectin‑3 as the primary binding target; competitive binding and DARTS confirmed functional interaction; combined in vivo (rat MI) and in vitro (TGF‑β1‑stimulated CFs) models demonstrated antifibrotic efficacy. - Clinical Angle: Galectin‑3 is a known prognostic marker in heart failure; Iminostilbene’s dual anti‑fibrotic and anti‑inflammatory/oxidative properties suggest potential as a novel therapeutic agent for post‑MI remodeling. - Limitations: Findings are preclinical (rat and cell culture); human data, long‑term safety, pharmacokinetics, and off‑target effects remain unaddressed.
