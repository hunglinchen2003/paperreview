🎯 Executive Summary

1. A novel C‑glycosylated thiodisaccharide scaffold was shown to bind Galectin‑7 and the N‑terminal domain of Galectin‑8 with high affinity, revealing key structural determinants for selective recognition. 2. Intratumoral ^198Au nanoparticle brachytherapy in a 4T1 triple‑negative breast cancer model not only suppressed tumor growth and lung metastasis but also markedly down‑regulated Galectin‑3 expression, linking radiotherapy to modulation of this pro‑metastatic lectin. 3. Systematic review data confirm that elevated circulating Galectin‑3 predicts cardiotoxicity across chemotherapy and immune checkpoint inhibitor regimens, acting through TGF‑β/Smad activation, oxidative stress, and NF‑κB‑mediated inflammation. 4. A PRISMA‑ScR scoping review uncovered a complete absence of serial blood Galectin‑1/3 measurements during prostate radiotherapy, highlighting a critical biomarker gap despite mature data on testosterone, IL‑6, and γ‑H2AX. 5. Additional studies on post‑Golgi membrane damage provide context for galectin‑mediated cellular stress responses but lack direct translational focus.
