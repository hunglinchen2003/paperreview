1. Charge‑switching ionizable lipids (S‑lipids) dramatically reduce lipid nanoparticle (LNP) toxicity by suppressing Galectin‑8 signaling, offering a safer platform for mRNA delivery. 2. The Japanese flounder C‑GRP lacks canonical carbohydrate‑binding residues and glycan affinity, revealing a non‑lectin galectin that may modulate immunity through alternative mechanisms. 3. Human intervertebral disc degeneration is driven by Galectin‑4, which promotes extracellular matrix remodeling and inflammatory signaling in degenerative discs. 4. Serum Galectin‑3 predicts heart failure incidence and all‑cause mortality in chronic kidney disease patients, underscoring its role as a cardio‑renal risk biomarker. 5. Grass carp CiGal‑4 contains four CRDs; a 2‑CRD truncation exhibits superior bactericidal activity, highlighting domain coordination as key to antimicrobial potency.
