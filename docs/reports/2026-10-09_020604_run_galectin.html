1. Galectin-3 於化療心臟毒性之預測（Galectin-3 於多柔比星誘導心臟功能障礙之預測指標）

  • 核心機制：多柔比星誘發的氧化壓力與細胞凋亡可上調Galectin‑3表達，Galectin‑3進一步促進心肌纖維化與炎症，導致心肌功能下降。
  • 實驗亮點：前瞻性隊列設計，連續三個時間點（治療前、循環2後、循環4後）同步測定血漿Galectin‑3與心臟超音波；使用GLS作為敏感的心肌功能指標，避免僅依賴LVEF。
  • 臨床啟示：Galectin‑3於循環4後可作為高敏感度（92.9%）的早期預測指標，結合GLS可提升早期偵測子臨床心臟毒性；可用於篩選需加強心臟保護策略（如ACEI/ARB、β‑阻滯劑）之高危患者。
  • 研究限制：樣本量有限（55人），單中心設計，缺乏對照組；未進行長期追蹤；未測定其他心臟毒性相關生物標記物；未探討Galectin‑3上調的分子機制。

2. Galectin 研究動態（—）

核心機制：早期復發主要由於腫瘤細胞在移植前已具備高度惡性染色體異常，導致免疫監視失效；晚期復發則多由於移植後免疫環境調節失衡或微小腫瘤存留。 實驗亮點：利用染色體分析比較診斷與復發時的變化，發現早期復發患者染色體變化更頻繁，說明疾病進化速度快。 臨床啟示：早期復發患者需考慮更積極的預防性治療或更頻繁的監測；晚期復發患者則可接受傳統化療或再移植，預後較佳。

3. 毒液誘導 MSC 免疫調節功能提升（M. eupeus 全毒液及其 F1 分離物對人臍帶 MSC 表達 Galectin‑1、3、8 及其免疫調節功能的影響）

  • 實驗亮點：同時評估基因表達與功能性 sEV 介導的 Treg 生成，並使用多個分離物對比，確定 F1 為關鍵活性成分。

4. KSHV 透過 Galectin‑1 促進 PDGFRA 信號（KSHV 介導的 MSC N‑糖基化重塑與 Galectin‑1 介導的 PDGFRA 激活）

🧬 Cross-Study Synthesis & Molecular Mechanisms

Mechanism and evidence threads across papers on «galectin»:

1. Galectin-3 Cardiotoxicity Prediction (Galectin-3 as a predictor of doxorubicin-induced cardiac dysfunction)

  • Core Insight: Doxorubicin‑induced oxidative stress and apoptosis upregulate Galectin‑3, which promotes myocardial fibrosis and inflammation, leading to functional decline.
  • Methodology Highlights: Prospective cohort with serial measurements at baseline, cycle‑2, and cycle‑4; simultaneous assessment of plasma Galectin‑3 and echocardiographic parameters, using GLS as a sensitive functional marker.
  • Clinical Angle: Post‑cycle‑4 Galectin‑3 shows high sensitivity (92.9%) for early cardiotoxicity; combined with GLS improves subclinical detection and can guide cardioprotective interventions.
  • Limitations: Small sample size, single‑center, no control group, short follow‑up, lack of other cardiac biomarkers, and no mechanistic assays of Galectin‑3 up‑regulation.

2. Galectin Review (—)

Core Insight: Early relapse driven by aggressive cytogenetic abnormalities pre‑transplant that evade immune surveillance; late relapse often reflects post‑transplant immune dysregulation or residual disease. Methodology: Comparative karyotype analysis between diagnosis and relapse highlighted higher chromosomal change rates in early relapse. Clinical Angle: Early relapse patients may benefit from intensified monitoring or pre‑emptive therapy, whereas late relapse patients respond better to conventional salvage regimens or re‑transplantation.

3. Venom‑augmented MSC immunomodulation (Effect of M. eupeus whole venom and F1 fraction on Galectin‑1/3/8 expression and immunomodulatory capacity of UC‑MSC)

  • Core Insight: F1 fraction and whole venom upregulate Galectin‑1/3/8 in MSCs, likely enhancing sEV‑mediated immunosuppression via TLR2/4‑IL‑10/TGF‑β pathways.
  • Methodology Highlights: Parallel assessment of gene expression and functional sEV‑induced Treg generation, with fraction comparison to pinpoint F1 as the active component.
  • Clinical Angle: Pre‑treating MSCs with F1 could boost their immunomodulatory potency for autologous MSC therapies or transplant tolerance.
  • Limitations: Single donor MSC source, lack of protein or functional assays, unclear direct role of Galectins in Treg induction, and no safety/toxicity assessment of venom components.

4. KSHV‑Galectin‑1 PDGFRA signaling (KSHV‑induced MSC N‑glycan remodeling and Galectin‑1 mediated PDGFRA activation)

Core Insight: KSHV remodels MSC surface N‑glycans by up‑regulating UDP‑GlcNAc transporters and branching enzymes (MGAT5, B3GNT2, B4GALT1) while down‑regulating sialidase NEU3, producing highly branched, low α(2‑6) sialylated glycans that avidly bind Galectin‑1. Galectin‑1 cross‑links PDGFRA, driving its phosphorylation and downstream MAPK/PI3K signaling to promote survival and proliferation. Methodology Highlights: Integrated transcriptomics with high‑throughput N‑glycan profiling; validation in Kaposi sarcoma patient samples; functional inhibition of Galectin‑1 reduces PDGFRA activation. Clinical Angle: Galectin‑1 or its glycan binding interface emerges as a therapeutic target; inhibition of branching enzymes (e.g., MGAT5) could dampen PDGFRA signaling, offering a novel strategy for Kaposi sarcoma treatment. Limitations: Lack of in vivo validation; no direct evidence of Galectin‑1/PDGFRA physical interaction; pharmacodynamics and toxicity of Galectin‑1 inhibitors remain unexplored.

5. Gal-3 as a potential biomarker in male infertility (Gal-3 expression in testicular tissue of idiopathic non‑obstructive azoospermia and its link to fibrosis and apoptosis)

  • Core Insight: Gal‑3 may drive testicular fibrosis via inflammatory cytokine release and collagen deposition, impairing spermatogenesis; it cooperates with caspase‑3 to promote germ cell apoptosis.
  • Methodology Highlights: Direct sampling from mTESE provides authentic patient tissue; IHC quantification of Gal‑3 and apoptosis indices coupled with Johnsen scoring offers a robust link between histology and molecular markers.
  • Clinical Angle: Elevated Gal‑3 could serve as a predictive biomarker for failed sperm retrieval in iNOA and a therapeutic target for anti‑fibrotic or anti‑inflammatory interventions.
  • Limitations: Small cohort (n=15) without normal controls; no mechanistic or functional assays; no longitudinal or interventional data; full methods inaccessible beyond abstract.
