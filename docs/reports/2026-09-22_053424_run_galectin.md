**核心機制**：Galectin-9透過其碳水化合物識別域(CRDs)結合腎小球基質細胞表面β-半乳糖苷，調節TGF‑β/Smad訊號通路，抑制基質細胞增殖與纖維化基因表達。
**實驗亮點**：1) 以乳糖作為CRD競爭抑制劑，證實Galectin‑9效應依賴於糖結合；2) 結合臨床隊列與體外實驗，將血漿水平與組織病理直接對照；3) 兩個獨立隊列（TRTOI、BKBC）驗證其預後價值。
**臨床啟示**：血漿Galectin‑9可作為非侵入性腎小球基質擴張指標，協助風險分層；若其抑制TGF‑β訊號的作用在體內亦成立，可能成為治療腎纖維化的新靶點。

### 2. 牙周炎菌 P. gingivalis 透過 Galectin‑3 促進心房纖維化（Galectin‑3/TGF‑β1 介導的心房纖維化機制）

- **臨床啟示**：牙周炎成為可調節的心房顫動風險因子；若能在早期介入牙周治療，或許能降低心房纖維化進程，減少心房顫動發生率。

### 4. 甲狀腺癌中 Galectin-3 的角色（Galectin-3 在 RAS‑類與 BRAF p.V600E‑類甲狀腺癌中的表達差異與臨床意涵）

- **實驗亮點**：利用組織微陣列進行大規模免疫組化，精準比較同一腺體內不同分子亞型腫瘤的 Galectin‑3 表達；統計分析顯示 BRAF p.V600E‑類腫瘤表達率顯著高於 RAS‑類，並與淋巴管侵襲及轉移相關聯。
- **臨床啟示**：Galectin‑3 可作為診斷工具，協助區分 BRAF p.V600E‑類與 RAS‑類甲狀腺癌；其高表達提示較高的轉移風險，應考慮更積極的手術與術後監測。

### 5. Galectin-14與肝癌免疫逃逸（Galectin-14在肝細胞癌中的免疫抑制作用）
• **核心機制**：Galectin‑14直接結合UDP‑葡萄糖焦磷酸酶2（UGP2），促進其聚合與酶活性，從而加速UDP‑糖供體（UDP‑Glc、UDP‑GlcNAc、UDP‑GlcA）合成，最終提升肝細胞癌細胞表面硫酸化肝素（HS）鏈的長度與密度。高水平HS在腫瘤微環境中形成黏附屏障，抑制CD8+ T細胞與腫瘤細胞的接觸，誘導T細胞耗竭。
• **實驗亮點**：1) 透過臨床HCC樣本與生存資料建立HS水平與患者預後的關聯；2) 利用體內外T細胞功能測試證實HS高表現導致T細胞耗竭；3) 轉錄組篩選HS‑高/低腫瘤克隆，定位Galectin‑14為上游調控因子；4) 在轉基因小鼠中敲除/過表達Galectin‑14，觀察腫瘤進展與免疫狀態變化；5) 進行代謝追蹤與蛋白互作實驗，證明Galectin‑14與UGP2的直接結合及其對UDP‑糖供體合成的影響。
• **臨床啟示**：Galectin‑14可作為HCC免疫逃逸的診斷標記；抑制Galectin‑14或其與UGP2的互作，或降低HS合成，可能恢復CD8+ T細胞功能，為HCC免疫治療提供新策略。

## 🧬 Cross-Study Synthesis & Molecular Mechanisms

Across these studies, galectins converge on carbohydrate‑dependent modulation of key signaling pathways. Galectin‑9 and Galectin‑3 both engage β‑galactoside receptors to dampen TGF‑β/Smad signaling in mesangial cells and atrial fibroblasts, respectively, illustrating a shared anti‑fibrotic or pro‑fibrotic axis depending on context. Galectin‑8’s role in preserving endosomal membrane integrity during AAV2 trafficking underscores a broader theme of galectins acting as sentinels of membrane stress, a property that may be harnessed to improve viral vector safety. In oncology, Galectin‑3 and Galectin‑14 exemplify divergent mechanisms: Galectin‑3 promotes invasion via ECM remodeling, while Galectin‑14 reprograms glycocalyx composition to shield tumor cells from cytotoxic T‑cells. The common thread is carbohydrate recognition driving downstream transcriptional and metabolic programs that dictate cell fate, invasion, and immune surveillance.

## 💡 Translational & Therapeutic Implications

Diagnostic implications are immediate: plasma Galectin‑9 offers a non‑invasive biomarker for mesangial injury and CKD progression; immunohistochemical Galectin‑3 and Galectin‑14 staining could refine risk stratification in thyroid and HCC patients, respectively. Therapeutically, lactose or small‑molecule CRD inhibitors may restore Galectin‑9’s anti‑fibrotic effect in the kidney, while Galectin‑3 antagonists (e.g., TD139) could mitigate atrial remodeling and thyroid tumor aggressiveness. In gene therapy, engineering AAV capsids to exploit the AAVR‑retromer pathway may enhance nuclear delivery while minimizing cytotoxicity. Finally, targeting Galectin‑14‑UGP2 interaction offers a novel metabolic checkpoint to re‑sensitize exhausted T‑cells in HCC, potentially synergizing with checkpoint blockade.

## 🔭 Future Perspectives & Open Research Questions

Key open questions include: (1) Can the anti‑fibrotic effect of Galectin‑9 be replicated in vivo and translated into a therapeutic agent? (2) What are the quantitative dynamics of *P. gingivalis* translocation and Galectin‑3 induction in human atria, and can periodontal treatment be rigorously tested in randomized trials? (3) How does Galectin‑8’s protective role influence long‑term safety of AAV vectors in diverse cell types? (4) What functional assays will confirm Galectin‑3’s mechanistic contribution to BRAF‑driven invasion, and can dual inhibition of MAPK and Galectin‑3 improve outcomes? (5) Does Galectin‑14 expression correlate with response to immunotherapy in HCC, and can small‑molecule inhibitors of the Galectin‑14‑UGP2 axis be developed? Addressing these will require longitudinal cohorts, advanced imaging of galectin dynamics, and early‑phase clinical trials.
