OGA activity modulates nutrient‑sensing pathways that converge on Gal‑3 transcription and post‑translational modification, linking metabolic stress to Gal‑3‑mediated inflammation. 3. Salivary Gal‑3 as an oral disease biomarker – Cross‑sectional analysis of 120 subjects revealed progressively higher salivary Gal‑3 concentrations from healthy controls to periodontitis, oral potentially malignant disorders (OPMDs), and oral cancer, suggesting Gal‑3’s role in chronic oral inflammation and malignant transformation. 4. Glia‑associated secretome in temporal lobe epilepsy (TLE) – Multi‑omics and CSF proteomics identified a Gal‑3‑containing secretome signature enriched for glial activation and metabolic dysregulation. Machine‑learning models achieved >85 % diagnostic accuracy distinguishing TLE patients from non‑epileptic controls. 5. Context‑selective galectin inhibition in oncology – A comprehensive review highlighted that current carbohydrate‑binding inhibitors fail to account for subcellular localization, tumor microenvironment, and disease stage. The authors propose combinatorial strategies that pair CRD blockade with cell‑type‑specific modulators and companion biomarkers to improve therapeutic efficacy.
