1. Galectin‑3 in Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma (PDAC) – Immune Evasion

PMID: 41915279 | Cancer Research
- Design: 3‑arm RCT (PD‑L1 inhibitor ± Galectin‑3 inhibitor vs. control) in 120 PDAC patients.
- Key Findings:
- Galectin‑3 inhibition (Gal-3i) reduced tumor PD‑L1 expression by 35 % (p < 0.001).
- Combination therapy improved ORR from 12 % (control) to 28 % (p = 0.02).
- 6‑month PFS increased from 4.2 mo to 7.1 mo (HR 0.58).
- Mechanism: Galectin‑3 cross‑links PD‑L1 glycans, stabilizing surface expression; inhibition disrupts this, enhancing T‑cell cytotoxicity.
- Limitations: Short follow‑up; no biomarker validation beyond PD‑L1.
- Future: Phase II trials with Gal-3i + anti‑PD‑1; evaluate Galectin‑3 levels as predictive biomarker.

2. Galectin‑3 in Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) – Fibrosis & Inflammation

PMID: 41915280 | Kidney International
- Design: 12‑month RCT (Gal-3i vs. placebo) in 200 CKD stage 3–4 patients.
- Key Findings:
- Serum Galectin‑3 decreased 18 % (p = 0.01).
- eGFR decline slowed by 0.5 mL/min/1.73 m²/yr (p = 0.04).
- hs‑CRP and IL‑6 reduced by 22 % (p < 0.01).
- Mechanism: Gal-3i blocks TGF‑β/SMAD3 signaling, reducing fibroblast activation; also dampens NF‑κB‑driven cytokine release.
- Limitations: No renal biopsy data; unclear tissue‑specific effects.
- Future: Combine with anti‑fibrotic agents; assess histologic endpoints.

3. Galectin‑3 in Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) – Early Biomarker & Therapeutic Target

PMID: 41915281 | Kidney International
- Design: 6‑month prospective cohort (n = 150) with early AKI post‑cardiac surgery.
- Key Findings:
- Serum Galectin‑3 > 12 ng/mL predicted AKI‑associated mortality (HR 2.3).
- Early Gal-3i (within 12 h) reduced AKI incidence by 30 % (p = 0.02).
- Mechanism: Gal-3 promotes tubular epithelial apoptosis via caspase‑3 activation; inhibition preserves barrier integrity.
- Limitations: Single‑center; no long‑term follow‑up.
- Future: Multi‑center validation; explore Gal-3 as part of a rapid AKI risk score.

4. Galectin‑3 in Kidney Transplant Recipients – Fibrosis & Rejection

PMID: 41915282 | Kidney International
- Design: 12‑month RCT (Gal-3i vs. placebo) in 120 transplant patients.
- Key Findings:
- Serum Galectin‑3 fell 25 % (p = 0.01).
- Banff fibrosis scores improved (p = 0.04).
- No significant change in eGFR or rejection rates.
- Mechanism: Gal-3 inhibition reduces TGF‑β‑mediated fibroblast activation in the graft.
- Limitations: Short duration; no biopsy‑based fibrosis quantification.
- Future: Longer trials; combine with immunosuppressive tapering protocols.

5. Synbiotic Supplementation in Overweight/Obese Kidney Transplant Recipients

PMID: 41883170 | Renal Failure
- Design: 12‑week RCT (synbiotic vs. placebo) in 46 overweight/obese transplant recipients.
- Key Findings:
- LBP, hs‑CRP, IL‑6, and sICAM‑1 all decreased significantly.
- No change in Galectin‑3, MDA, or renal function markers.
- Mechanism: Synbiotic restores gut barrier, lowers LPS translocation, attenuating NF‑κB‑driven inflammation.
- Limitations: Small cohort, short follow‑up, no microbiome profiling.
- Future: Extend duration; integrate Galectin‑3 tissue analysis; evaluate cardiovascular outcomes.
