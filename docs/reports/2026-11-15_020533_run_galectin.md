- **核心機制**：Galectin‑3作為糖尿病相關炎症介質，透過結合細胞表面糖類受體，促進巨噬細胞活化、纖維化及血管內皮功能障礙，進而加劇動脈粥樣硬化進程。

• **核心機制**：PROTAC 由糖模仿配體、E3 受體招募配體與連接子組成，形成三元複合物促進 Galectin-3 的泛素化與蛋白酶體降解。此機制突破傳統占據式抑制，實現事件驅動的目標清除。
• **實驗亮點**：文獻回顧中引用的最新研究報告，利用設計優化的連接子與高親和力糖模仿配體，達到低奈摩爾濃度下的選擇性降解，並證明了 PROTAC 能夠穿透細胞膜並定位於細胞內 Galectin-3。
• **臨床啟示**：若能有效降低細胞內 Galectin-3，可能改善其在腫瘤免疫、纖維化及心血管疾病中的病理作用。此策略亦可作為其他凝集素靶向治療的藍圖。
• **研究限制**：僅以文獻回顧為主，缺乏實驗數據；PROTAC 對細胞外 Galectin-3 的影響未知；連接子長度與化學性質對降解效率的影響仍需實驗驗證。

## 🧬 Cross-Study Synthesis & Molecular Mechanisms

Gal‑3, Gal‑1, and Gal‑8 converge on inflammation‑fibrosis‑oxidative stress axes. Gal‑3 drives macrophage activation and endothelial dysfunction in diabetic macrovascular disease, while PROTAC‑mediated degradation suggests that intracellular Gal‑3 is a key pro‑fibrotic driver in diverse pathologies. Gal‑1’s transcription‑protein dissociation under oxidative stress reveals a post‑translational regulatory mechanism that modulates neuronal plasticity genes (CREB1, MAPK1, DRD1/2) and may intersect with Gal‑3‑mediated cytokine release. Gal‑8’s rapid tissue‑specific up‑regulation and ROS induction in teleosts mirror mammalian innate immune lectin functions, underscoring a conserved role in pathogen‑induced oxidative defense. Shared pathways include NF‑κB activation, TGF‑β signaling, and ROS‑mediated apoptosis, positioning galectins as central nodes in disease‑specific inflammatory networks.

## 💡 Translational & Therapeutic Implications

The diagnostic utility of plasma Gal‑3 for early detection of macrovascular complications in T2DM could be integrated into risk calculators, especially when combined with the four‑protein panel. PROTACs targeting Gal‑3 open therapeutic avenues for fibrosis, cancer, and cardiovascular disease, offering event‑driven inhibition that may circumvent resistance seen with occupancy‑based inhibitors. In cardio‑oncology, baseline Gal‑3 levels, though not predictive of LVEF decline, could serve as a biomarker for patient stratification and monitoring of subclinical myocardial injury. Aquaculture may benefit from Gal‑8‑based immunomodulators to enhance disease resistance, while neuropsychiatric research could explore Gal‑1 neutralization or modulation to influence synaptic plasticity and antidepressant response. Each application requires rigorous validation, dosage optimization, and safety profiling.

## 🔭 Future Perspectives & Open Research Questions

Future studies must address the causal link between Gal‑3 elevation and macrovascular pathology through prospective, longitudinal cohorts and interventional trials. The extracellular versus intracellular roles of Gal‑3 remain to be delineated; PROTACs currently target only the intracellular pool, raising questions about the therapeutic impact on circulating Gal‑3. For Gal‑1, the mechanistic basis of transcription‑protein dissociation under oxidative stress warrants deeper investigation, potentially revealing novel targets for neuroprotection. Cross‑species comparisons of Gal‑8 function could inform translational strategies from fish to mammals. Additionally, combinatorial approaches—pairing Gal‑3 degradation with anti‑inflammatory or anti‑fibrotic agents—may yield synergistic benefits. Finally, the development of high‑affinity, selective galectin ligands and degraders will accelerate the translation of these findings into clinical and industrial settings.
