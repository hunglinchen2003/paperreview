3. CD28 is Not Required for T cell Activation.（CD28 is Not Required for T cell Activation.）

  • 核心內容： It has been almost four decades since CD28 was identified as a major player (co-stimulator) of T cell activation and has been recognized as one of the major milestones in the T cell field. Most importantly, the depletion of CD28 in mice did not lead to a major defect in T cell activation response, even when combined with the deletion of ICOS, a homolog of CD28.
