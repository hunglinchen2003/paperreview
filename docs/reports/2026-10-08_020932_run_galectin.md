OGA activity modulates nutrient‑sensing pathways that converge on Gal‑3 transcription and post‑translational modification, linking metabolic stress to Gal‑3‑mediated inflammation.
3. **Salivary Gal‑3 as an oral disease biomarker** – Cross‑sectional analysis of 120 subjects revealed progressively higher salivary Gal‑3 concentrations from healthy controls to periodontitis, oral potentially malignant disorders (OPMDs), and oral cancer, suggesting Gal‑3’s role in chronic oral inflammation and malignant transformation.
