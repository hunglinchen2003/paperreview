## 🧬 跨文獻深度剖析與分子機轉總結

- **核心機制**：CLC由嗜酸性粒細胞分泌的Galectin‑10結晶聚集，形成局部型2炎症微環境，誘發嗜酸性粒細胞、嗜中性粒細胞及外部網狀陷阱的聚集，進一步加劇氣道阻塞。  - **實驗亮點**：① 透過配對活檢證明CLC與炎症細胞共定位；② 小鼠模型證實CLC沉積可持續造成氣道阻塞，且抗Galectin‑10抗體能溶解結晶、減輕阻塞與炎症；③ 開發針對CLC的PET探針，實現非侵入性可視化。  - **臨床啟示**：CLC可作為急性ABPA的診斷生物標記；抗Galectin‑10治療或結晶溶解策略可能成為新型氣道阻塞治療；CLC‑PET可用於疾病監測與治療反應評估。  - **研究限制**：僅針對急性ABPA，樣本量有限；缺乏長期臨床干預數據；小鼠模型可能無法完全模擬人類氣道環境；未提供PMCID，全文可獲取受限。

## 🧬 Cross-Study Synthesis & Molecular Mechanisms

Galectin‑10, Galectin‑3, and Galectin‑9 illustrate a spectrum of extracellular galectin functions across distinct pathologies. In the airways, Galectin‑10 crystals form a localized type‑2 inflammatory niche that recruits eosinophils and neutrophils, mirroring the microglial‑driven glial‑program signature seen in neuropathic pain where Galectin‑3 sustains chronic neuroinflammation. Both Galectin‑10 and Galectin‑3 act as surface ligands for glycan‑dependent receptors (e.g., CD44, PD‑1), modulating cell‑cell communication and apoptosis. Galectin‑9’s interaction with the amino‑acid transporter SLC1A5 exemplifies a metabolic axis, distinct yet complementary to the inflammatory circuits mediated by Galectin‑3. Shared pathways include glycan recognition, receptor clustering, and downstream signaling that culminates in altered cellular phenotype—whether it be mucus plug formation, pain sensitization, immune senescence, or tumor metabolism.

## 💡 Translational & Therapeutic Implications

The translational impact of these findings is multifold:
- **Diagnostics**: CLC‑PET offers non‑invasive imaging of mucus plugs; Galectin‑3 surface expression could serve as a flow‑cytometry marker for activated/memory T cells in chronic inflammation.
- **Therapeutics**: Anti‑Galectin‑10 antibodies and Galectin‑3 inhibitors (Lumogallion, emerging small molecules) show promise in reducing airway obstruction and neuropathic pain, respectively. Galectin‑9 neutralizing antibodies and SLC1A5 inhibitors provide a novel metabolic blockade in HCC.
- **Biomarker development**: LGALS3 expression correlates with pain severity and keloid fibrosis, offering a target for monitoring disease progression and therapeutic response.
- **Clinical trials**: Early‑phase studies are warranted to evaluate safety, target engagement, and efficacy of galectin‑directed agents across these indications.

## 🔭 Future Perspectives & Open Research Questions

Future research should prioritize: 1) Longitudinal human studies to validate Galectin‑10 as a predictive biomarker for ABPA relapse and to assess antibody therapy in larger cohorts; 2) Genetic knockout/overexpression models of Galectin‑3 to dissect its causal role in neuroimmune remodeling and T‑cell exhaustion; 3) Translational studies linking Galectin‑3‑mediated senescence to keloid recurrence and potential senolytic interventions; 4) Clinical evaluation of Galectin‑9/SLC1A5 axis inhibitors in HCC patients, including biomarker‑guided patient selection; 5) Integration of multi‑omics (scRNA‑seq, spatial transcriptomics, proteomics) to map galectin‑dependent networks across tissues. Addressing these gaps will clarify galectin biology and accelerate therapeutic translation.
