🎯 Executive Summary

1. Dapagliflozin cardioprotection – In a 94‑patient phase‑II trial, dapagliflozin 10 mg daily improved 4‑month LVEF by 2.40 % (p<0.001) and preserved diastolic function (E/A ratio decline attenuated, p=0.030). Galectin‑3 fell significantly versus placebo (p<0.001), while NT‑proBNP rise was blunted (p=0.004). 2. Biliary atresia transcriptomics – Integrated single‑cell and bulk‑tissue analysis of 119,538 liver cells revealed a pronounced epithelial‑stromal inflammatory milieu. Key fibro‑inflammatory pathways, including galectin‑mediated adhesion and cytokine signaling, were up‑regulated in cholangiocytes and activated fibroblasts. 3. LGALS3‑driven hepatic macrophage phenotype – Single‑cell profiling identified a pro‑inflammatory, low‑efferocytic macrophage subset marked by LGALS3, which promotes hepatic stellate cell activation via ANXA2 signaling. 4. Retraction of galectin‑3 AVF study – The original AVF stenosis paper was retracted due to data integrity concerns, underscoring the need for rigorous validation of galectin‑3 as a vascular biomarker. 5. Kidney function dynamics – Post‑ICU survivors exhibited that high variability in serum creatinine, independent of absolute levels, correlated with increased mortality, suggesting that dynamic biomarkers may better capture renal stress than static values.
