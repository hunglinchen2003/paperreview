1. Gal‑3/CaV1.2 互作提供一條全新血壓調控靶點，可能開發出更具選擇性且副作用較低的抗高血壓藥物； 2. iGal3BP 與 Gal‑1 模擬肽的聯合使用示範了多靶點協同療法的可行性，對於難治性高血壓患者具有潛在價值。

🧬 Cross-Study Synthesis & Molecular Mechanisms

Across these studies, Galectin‑3 emerges as a central hub linking Ca²⁺‑dependent signaling, extracellular matrix (ECM) remodeling, and immune cell polarization. In renal fibrosis, Gal‑3 promotes M1/M2 macrophage crosstalk via MALAT1‑mediated exosomal transfer, while in hypertension it directly enhances CaV1.2 channel trafficking, amplifying smooth‑muscle Ca²⁺ influx. In melanoma, Gal‑3’s carbohydrate recognition domain scaffolds ECM components, fostering CAF and MDSC immunosuppression. Nicotine‑induced CHRNA3 activation also converges on Ca²⁺ overload, suggesting a shared calcium‑dependent vulnerability that Gal‑3 can exploit or counteract. These convergent pathways underscore Gal‑3’s versatility as both a mediator and a therapeutic target across diverse pathologies.

💡 Translational & Therapeutic Implications

The mechanistic insights translate into tangible clinical avenues. Melatonin or its analogues could be repurposed as Gal‑3‑targeted antifibrotic agents, with exosomal MALAT1 serving as a non‑invasive biomarker for renal fibrosis progression. The engineered peptide iGal3BP, which disrupts Gal‑3/CaV1.2 interaction, offers a novel antihypertensive strategy that may outperform conventional calcium‑channel blockers and ACE inhibitors in preclinical models. In oncology, Gal‑3 inhibitors (e.g., TD139, GR-MD-02) could be combined with PD‑1/PD‑L1 checkpoint blockade to remodel the tumor microenvironment, enhancing T‑cell infiltration and cytotoxicity. Finally, CHRNA3 antagonists or PLA2G4A inhibitors may mitigate e‑cigarette‑induced airway autophagic dysfunction, providing a therapeutic niche for smokers and vaping populations.

🔭 Future Perspectives & Open Research Questions

Key questions remain: (1) What are the long‑term safety, immunogenicity, and pharmacokinetics of iGal3BP and other Gal‑3‑blocking peptides in humans? (2) Can melatonin’s Gal‑3‑mediated antifibrotic effects be replicated in chronic human renal disease, and is MALAT1 a reliable predictive biomarker? (3) How does Gal‑3 interact with other galectins (e.g., Gal‑1, Gal‑9) in the ECM‑immune axis, and could combinatorial inhibition yield synergistic benefits? (4) What is the clinical relevance of nicotine‑CHRNA3‑PLA2G4A‑autophagy signaling in human airway disease, and can Gal‑3 modulation influence this pathway? Addressing these will require phase I/II trials, longitudinal biomarker studies, and integrative multi‑omics approaches.
