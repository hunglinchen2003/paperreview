### 1. 膜環境導向的肽選擇與納米載體靶向（P1肽與Galectin-1相關的膜環境介導的納米載體靶向傳遞）

## 🧬 Cross-Study Synthesis & Molecular Mechanisms

Across these studies, Galectin‑1 emerges as a central mediator of tumor‑cell interaction and immune modulation. The P1 peptide exploits Galectin‑1’s membrane‑associated glycan recognition to drive nanocarrier endocytosis, while MSC‑EV‑derived Galectin‑1 dampens TGF‑β/Smad signaling through JAK‑STAT, promoting tissue repair. Galectin‑3, though distinct, intersects with Galectin‑1 pathways by regulating epithelial migration and cytokine release; α‑lactose antagonism of Galectin‑3 restores barrier function, suggesting a shared lectin‑dependent checkpoint in inflammation. Galectin‑9, acting through Tim‑3, induces T‑cell exhaustion; its blockade synergizes with metabolic reprogramming (SLC25A51) to restore effector function. Together, these findings delineate a lectin‑centric axis—Galectin‑1/3/9—that coordinates cellular uptake, immune polarization, and metabolic fitness in disease contexts.

## 💡 Translational & Therapeutic Implications

The P1 peptide offers a blueprint for designing Galectin‑1‑targeted nanomedicines, potentially improving drug delivery in triple‑negative breast cancer. Standardizing Galectin‑1 content in MSC‑EV preparations could serve as a potency metric for regenerative therapies in intrauterine adhesions, while local hydrogel delivery may extend residence time. Nutraceutical modulation of gut immunity via PFP size‑dependent TLR and Galectin‑3 pathways opens avenues for functional foods that enhance epithelial barrier integrity. In oncology, combining Galectin‑9 inhibitors with metabolic reprogramming (SLC25A51 mRNA‑LNP) could potentiate adoptive T‑cell therapies, especially in metabolically hostile tumors like pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. These translational strategies hinge on rigorous potency assays, biodistribution profiling, and safety evaluations.
